How to split a bill

  1. On the home screen, tap Scan Receipt, Choose Photo or Enter Manually.
  2. Check the items and prices, fix anything the scan misread, and add or remove lines.
  3. Add everyone at the table by first name.
  4. Tap each item, then tap who had it. Shared items split evenly; Everyone assigns it to the whole table.
  5. Set tax (amount or percent) and tip. Tax and tip are shared in proportion to what each person ordered.
  6. See each person's total, share the summary, or save it to History.

Common questions

Why don't the shares look perfectly equal? Totals are rounded to the cent so they always add up exactly to the receipt. Choose how leftover cents are handled in Settings → Leftover cents.

The scan missed an item. Lay the receipt flat in good light, or just add the line on the review screen.
