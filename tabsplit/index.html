Tabsplit Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Tabsplit is published by Securityinfinity Inc. This page explains what the app stores, why, and what you can do about it. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising or for tracking you across other apps and websites.

What the app stores

  • Email address, only if the user chooses to sign in, used for the account
  • User content: split titles, line item names and prices, and the first names the user enters, stored on the device and synced only when signed in

You can use Tabsplit without an account. In that case what you enter stays on your device. If you create an account, with an email code, Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google, your data is also saved to our servers so it is available on your other devices. Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real email.

Purchases

Tabsplit is free. It has no purchases, subscriptions or ads.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts the database and sign-in for accounts.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and, on iPhone and iPad, distributes the app.
  • Google provides Sign in with Google and, on Android, distributes the app.

These companies handle the data only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

Data saved on your device stays until you delete it or remove the app. Data saved to your account stays until you delete the account.

Deleting your account and data

Open Settings in the app and choose "Delete account and data". This permanently deletes your account and everything saved to it. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

Children

Tabsplit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@securityinfinity.com
