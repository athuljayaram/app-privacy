Tabsplit Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Tabsplit is published by Securityinfinity Inc. In short: Tabsplit does not collect any data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

The splits you create (split names, line items and prices, the first names you add, tax and tip settings) and your preferences (currency symbol, default tip, rounding, appearance). This is stored only on your device. We have no servers that receive it, and there is no account or sign-in.

Receipt photos

When you scan or choose a receipt photo, the text is read on your device using Apple's built-in text recognition. The photo is not uploaded anywhere and is not kept by the app after the items are read. Tabsplit asks for camera access only when you choose to scan, and uses the system photo picker, so it sees only the photo you pick.

Sharing

If you tap Share on a result, Tabsplit hands a plain-text summary to the iOS share sheet, and you choose where it goes. Nothing is sent otherwise.

No tracking, ads or analytics

Tabsplit contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code, and makes no network requests for its features. It has no purchases or subscriptions.

Deleting your data

Delete individual splits from History, or use Settings → Manage saved splits. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

Tabsplit does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Questions: support@securityinfinity.com
