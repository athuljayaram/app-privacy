Common questions

  • How do I log a drink? Tap the big button on the home screen. Each tap adds one glass of your chosen size.
  • I logged too much. How do I undo it? Open today's entries and swipe or tap an entry to edit or remove it.
  • How do I change my goal, glass size or units? Open Settings and change the daily goal, glass size, and ml or oz.
  • How does the streak work? Every day you reach your goal adds one to your streak. Missing a day resets it.
  • Where is my data stored? On your device. Deleting the app deletes your log.
